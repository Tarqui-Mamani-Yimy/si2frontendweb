## Brand & Style

### Personality & Purpose
The design system positions NexaCoop as an institutional yet agile financial ecosystem engineered for modern cooperative banking. It bridges fiduciary solidity, multi-tenant administrative security, and cutting-edge fintech ergonomics. The interface evokes unyielding trust, operational clarity, and forward-looking financial intelligence.

### Target Audience
- **Executives & Directors**: Cooperatives seeking rapid executive health monitoring, real-time liquidity indicators, and member growth analytics.
- **Credit Officers & Tellers**: Front-office operators requiring rapid keyboard navigation, dense yet clear operational tables, and error-proof transaction flows.
- **Compliance & Auditors**: Superadministrators auditing tenant status, RBAC role-permissions grids, and immutability logs.

### Aesthetic Direction: Modern Institutional Fintech
A synthesis of **Corporate Modernism** and **Tactile Precision**:
- Crisp, content-first layouts running on structured slate neutrals (`#0B132B`, `#0F172A`, `#F8FAFC`).
- Rich, authoritative emerald forest green base anchors primary institutional identity.
- High-efficiency vibrant mint accenting indicates operational readiness, live status, and positive fiscal trends.
- Subtle inner glows, crisp hairline borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`), and optical elevation layers replace flat, uninspired interfaces.

## Colors

### Hierarchy & Role Distribution
- **Primary Brand Anchors (`#064E3B` / `#0F5132`)**: Used across global navigation shells, persistent executive sidebars, high-priority buttons, active states, and formal document headers. Establishes institutional gravitas.
- **Fintech Accent (`#10B981` / `#34D399`)**: Digital mint brings vitality. Used exclusively for positive delta KPIs, active status indicators, progress tracks, and primary callout micro-badges.
- **Canvas & Surface Architecture**:
  - `canvas`: `#F8FAFC` (ultra-clean cool slate surface, preventing eye fatigue across long data sessions).
  - `surface-elevated`: `#FFFFFF` (pristine card and modal surfaces).
  - `surface-sidebar`: `#063D2E` to `#042C21` (deep, focused forest gradient replacing legacy flat panels).
  - `border-hairline`: `#E2E8F0` on white surfaces; `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` on dark sidebars.
- **Functional & Semantic Scale**:
  - `success`: `#059669` (Dark Mint/Emerald) / Soft fill `#ECFDF5`.
  - `warning`: `#D97706` (Warm Ochre) / Soft fill `#FFFBEB`.
  - `danger / error`: `#DC2626` (Precision Crimson) / Soft fill `#FEF2F2`.
  - `info`: `#0284C7` (Corporate Azure) / Soft fill `#F0F9FF`.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings
- **Display & Section Titles (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Used for view titles, high-level fiscal figures, and brand anchor components. Its geometric balance brings a friendly yet highly competent character to financial headers.
- **Operational Data & Body (Inter)**: Handles complex tabular layouts, micro-data, inputs, and system logs. Open counters and high x-height guarantee readability at 11px and 12px within dense audit and ledger tables.
- **Numeric Precision (`label-numeric`)**: Financial balances, member totals, and NIT codes apply tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure zero jitter during real-time dashboard updates.

## Layout & Spacing

### System Architecture
The application employs an asymmetric application shell:
- **Navigation Rail / Permanent Sidebar**: Fixed `264px` on desktop viewport, collapsing into an overlay slideout (`w-72`) on tablet and mobile viewports (`< 1024px`).
- **Main Workspace Canvas**: Fluid container with a max constraint of `1600px` for ultra-wide monitors, preserving horizontal scan discipline.

### Grid Rhythm & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12-column layout, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 2rem`. Analytical metric cards sit on a 4-column span (`grid-cols-4`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1.5rem`. Metric cards collapse to 2 columns (`grid-cols-2`), tables enforce horizontal touch scroll with pinned action bars.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout, `gutter: 0.75rem`, `margin: 1rem`. Form layouts collapse from side-by-side configurations into stacked vertical cards.

## Elevation & Depth

### Tonal Elevation & Micro-Outlines
Depth in this design system avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows in favor of crisp financial boundaries and subtle ambient light:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Flat `#F8FAFC`, zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Metric Containers, Table Wrappers)**: Background `#FFFFFF`, outline `1px solid #E2E8F0`, shadow `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Interactive Cards, Filter Drawers)**: Outline `1px solid #CBD5E1`, shadow `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`, vertical translation `-1px`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Dropdowns)**: Outline `1px solid #E2E8F0`, shadow `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Sidebar Depth**: Deep emerald gradient background with an internal edge border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` and an active navigation item inner glow (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Input Fields, Dropdowns & Buttons**: `8px` (`0.5rem`). Delivers high precision without harshness.
- **Cards, Modals & Data Tables**: `12px` to `16px` (`0.75rem` - `1rem`). Provides structured framing for analytical and data-heavy views.
- **Status Badges, Chips & Avatars**: Full pill `9999px`. Maximizes semantic distinction from square data cells.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Background `#064E3B`, text `#FFFFFF`, font-weight `600`, border `1px solid #04382A`. Hover: `#0F5132`, subtle upward lift. Active: `#03261C`.
- **Fintech Accent CTA**: Background `#10B981`, text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#059669`. Used for pivotal financial approvals and creation workflows (e.g., "+ Nueva Cooperativa").
- **Secondary / Outlined**: Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Hover: `#F1F5F9`, border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Destructive Ghost**: Text `#DC2626`, hover background `#FEF2F2`.

### Metric & KPI Cards
- White background (`#FFFFFF`) with `12px` radius and `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- Top row: Uppercase tracking label (`label-caps`, `#64748B`) paired with an icon housed in an emerald-tinted container (`#ECFDF5`, icon color `#064E3B`).
- Central metric: Displayed in `headline-lg` (`#0F172A`, tabular numbers).
- Bottom row: Micro-badge indicating percentage change (+12.4% with green trend arrow) vs previous period.

### Interactive Data Tables
- **Container**: Card wrapper with rounded corners (`12px`), preventing row outlines from bleeding.
- **Header**: `#F8FAFC` background, text in `table-header` (`#64748B`, uppercase, letter-spacing `0.05em`), border bottom `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Rows**: Alternating subtle states or clean white with `hover:bg-slate-50/80` transition. Height `52px` for comfortable visual rhythm.
- **Inline Action Buttons**: Grouped right-aligned flex containers with outlined micro-buttons ("Editar", "Desactivar").

### Filter Toolbar & Search Inputs
- Seamless horizontal bar above tables.
- Left side: Global omni-search input with leading search icon and instant-clear token.
- Right side: Segmented status dropdowns ("Todos los estados", "Activos", "Suspendidos") with chevron indicator and filter counter badges.

### Status Badges & Chips
- Soft-tint styling: `8px` horizontal padding, `2px` vertical padding, font-weight `600`, uppercase `11px`.
- `ACTIVO`: Background `#DCFCE7`, text `#15803D`, optional pulsing status dot (`#22C55E`).
- `PENDIENTE / REVISIÓN`: Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`.
- `INACTIVO / SUSPENDIDO`: Background `#FEE2E2`, text `#B91C1C`.

### Form Controls & RBAC Grids
- **Input Fields**: Height `40px`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, focus ring `2px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)` with border `#10B981`.
- **Role Permission Matrix**: Clear visual division between role categories and granular operational permissions. Checkboxes utilize custom emerald fill with white checkmarks on active state.
